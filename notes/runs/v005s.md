## What changed from v004

- Bigger jobs: one skill, make_wooden_pickaxe, does planks, sticks, table, place, pickaxe. 12 decisions became 6.
- 4 logs instead of 3: 13 planks pay for two tables.
- mine_stone digs 11 cobblestone in one call (3 pickaxe, 8 furnace).
- make_stone_pickaxe puts a second table down where the stone is (when none within 8 blocks), then makes the stone pickaxe and the furnace. This removes the 75 second walk back to the first table.
- Coal and iron come last, one dig each, target 1. A dig that "picked nothing up" is retried, not reported as a failure.
- If the table cannot be placed (run 27: bot among leaves), collect one more log to move, then retry.
- A `rest` skill once furnace, coal and iron are held, so the bot does not explore and die. Explore is hidden then.
- Briefing: same short ordered text as v004, goal reworded.
