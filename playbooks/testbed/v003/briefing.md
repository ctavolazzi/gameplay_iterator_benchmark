Goal: craft the pick. You need 3 wood and 1 stone in total.
Choose in this order:
1. If craft:pick is offered, choose it. It wins the game.
2. If craft:table is offered, choose it.
3. If you have fewer than 3 wood and get_wood is offered, choose it.
4. If you have no stone and get_stone is offered, choose it.
5. Otherwise explore in a direction you have not just come from, to find trees or stones.
Never go back and forth between the same two places. The skills avoid pits for you; do not worry about them.
