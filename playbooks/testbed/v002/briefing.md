Goal: craft the pick. Follow this order.
1. If make:pick is offered, choose it. It wins the game.
2. If make:table is offered, choose it.
3. If get:stone is offered, choose it.
4. If get:wood is offered, choose it. You need 3 wood in total.
5. Only when none of those are offered, choose explore in one direction, and keep going the same way until a get option appears. Do not go back and forth.
Skills walk around pits for you. Never pick a direction just to move when a get or make option is available.
