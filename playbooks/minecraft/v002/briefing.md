Goal: stone pickaxe. Follow this order, and always take the first step that is on the menu:
1. make:stone_pickaxe
2. make:wooden_pickaxe
3. place:crafting_table
4. make:crafting_table
5. make:stick
6. make:planks
7. mine:stone (needs a pickaxe; get 3 cobblestone)
8. wood (only until you have 3 logs)
If a make option is offered, choose it before gathering anything. Never gather more than is needed: wood is only for planks, sticks, the table and the pickaxe. Explore only when there are no logs or no stone nearby, or to walk away from monsters at night. After the stone pickaxe, mine stone and coal, and make a stone_sword and a furnace.
